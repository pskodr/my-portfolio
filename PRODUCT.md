## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating the owner for product management roles. They are skimming many candidates and need to quickly judge product sense, design thinking, and the ability to build.

Secondary: anyone curious about the owner as a person. The site should also convey personality and interests, not just credentials.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that shows the owner's strength in building products and in design thinking, and gives a real sense of who they are. Success: a recruiter leaves understanding how the owner thinks about products, has seen evidence of it in real projects, can find the resume and experience, and knows how to get in touch.

## Positioning

A product manager who builds: coding projects are presented as product work (problem, decisions, trade-offs, outcome), not just as code. Personality and passions are shown interactively rather than listed.

## Operating Context

Visitors usually arrive from a job application, LinkedIn, or a resume link, often with limited time. They look for the resume, experience, and project evidence, then contact information.
